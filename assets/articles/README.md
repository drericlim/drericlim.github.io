# Article pictures

Pictures used in patient guides, articles and Thoracic Reflections.

## In this folder

- `single-keyhole-surgery-stone.jpg`, `personalised-care-stone.jpg`, `your-consultation-stone.jpg`, `your-operation-stone.jpg`: the round picture in the header of each patient guide. `testimonials-stone.jpg` is the same for the Testimonials page. **All except `your-operation-stone.jpg` are stand-ins**, cropped from photos already on the site, until Professor Lim supplies a picture for each page. `your-operation-stone.jpg` (16 Nov 2026) is his own choice: a photo of him operating in the thoracic theatre, cropped around his head.

## Header pictures for patient guides

Each patient guide shows one round picture set in the stone at the top of the page. Name it with the page's address followed by `-stone`, e.g. `your-recovery-stone.jpg`. It must be square, at least 800 by 800, with the subject in the middle (the corners are cut off by the circle). It should be particular to the page and not a portrait of Professor Lim (a real photo of him at work, chosen by him, is fine, as on Your operation; AI pictures must never show him). A guide with no picture shows a plain stone.

## Naming

Start with the article's web address, then what the picture shows, e.g. `day-case-thoracic-surgery-ward.jpg`.

Always use lower case, with hyphens instead of spaces, and no other punctuation. To replace a picture, upload a new file **with exactly the same name**: the site picks it up automatically. Keep each file under 500 KB if you can.

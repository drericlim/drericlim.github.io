# Logo and site icons

Professor Lim's official lung logo, supplied by him on 27 October 2026, in a dark and a light version, plus the website's icons made from it.

## The logo

| File | What it is | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| `logo-on-dark.svg` | **Dark version**: white upper lobes, light and mid blue lower lobes, white line and ring. Transparent background. | Anything with a dark navy or photo background. It is shown beside Professor Lim's name at the top left of every page. |
| `logo-on-light.svg` | **Light version**: navy upper lobes, mid blue and light blue lower lobes, fine navy line and ring. Transparent background. | Anything with a white or pale background. |
| `logo-on-dark-1200.png`, `logo-on-light-1200.png` | The same two logos as pictures, 1200 pixels wide, transparent background. | Word documents, slides, emails and anything that cannot use `.svg` files. |
| `logo-dark-tile.svg` | The dark version centred on a navy square. | The source of the site icons below. |
| `logo-dark-original.png`, `logo-light-original.png` | The two images exactly as Professor Lim supplied them. | Reference only. |

The `.svg` files are sharp drawings traced from the originals, so they stay crisp at any size. Website pages should use the `.svg` files.

Colours: navy `#0E1D61`, mid blue `#0971CE`, light blue `#9DBAEA`, white `#FFFFFF`.

## Site icons (made from the dark version on navy)

- `../../favicon.ico` (at the top of the repository): the small icon in browser tabs and bookmarks (16, 32 and 48 pixels).
- `favicon-32.png`: a sharper copy of the tab icon for modern browsers.
- `apple-touch-icon.png` (180 by 180): shown when someone adds the site to an iPhone or iPad home screen.
- `icon-192.png`, `icon-512.png`: for Android home screens.

The tab icons fill more of the square than the phone icons, so the lungs stay readable at tiny sizes. To change the icons, ask Claude to remake the whole set from `logo-dark-tile.svg` or a new logo.

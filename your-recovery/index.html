  1. Part1

    Keeping active

    Exercise as much as you can (brisk walks, cycling, swimming), and do not stop when you get short of breath but push on a bit more each day.

  2. Part2

    Pain relief

    Aches and pains can take 4 to 6 weeks to settle. Reduce your painkillers gradually, usually no faster than one tablet a day.

    Most painkillers cause constipation, so drink plenty of fluids. If you cannot open your bowels, contact the hospital team for advice, or ask your GP.

  3. Part3

    Your wound

    Wash your wound with soap and water to keep it clean.

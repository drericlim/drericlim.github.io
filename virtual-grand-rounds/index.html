03How to join

Free to join, wherever you are.

  1. Register once

    Sign up for a free place and you will receive the joining details for the series.

  2. Join live on Friday

    Each lecture runs from 08:00 to 08:30 UK time, so it fits before the working day begins.

  3. 1 CME point per session

    Each session is approved for 1 CME point towards your continuing professional development.

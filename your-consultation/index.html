Your referral

An NHS consultation begins with a referral from your hospital’s multidisciplinary team. Once we receive it, we process the information and send you an appointment letter.

I also reserve an operation date for you in the first available slot, so that a place is already held if you decide to proceed to surgery.

Private patients

Private patients are self-funding and can make an appointment directly, by calling my office on +44 (0) 207 352 8121 or sending a message. I do not work with insurance companies. My office can give you a quote for your consultation, and for any treatment, before you book.

Before we meet

If time permits, we send you an invitation letter and a health questionnaire. Completing it in advance, including the medicines you take and their doses, means our time together can be spent on discussion.

Clinics are held at the Royal Brompton Hospital, and a typical appointment lasts 30 minutes. Please bring your completed self-assessment leaflet. If you have not been able to complete it, bring at least a list of your medicines and, ideally, of your past illnesses.

Who will be in the clinic

NHS clinics can be busy. The people usually present are Maria, my practice manager, who helps with administration and admission; a clinical nurse specialist, who is there to support you; and a research nurse, who can tell you about any research you may be able to take part in. Visiting doctors and medical students may also attend.

If you would prefer visiting doctors and students not to be present, please tell Maria and we will make sure they are not. This will not affect your consultation or your care in any way.

During the consultation

I will ask about your health and examine you. I will then summarise your condition and we will discuss the options. I always welcome your questions.

After the consultation

Once we have decided together on the best course, I will give you the consent forms you need: for surgery, if you have chosen it, and for any research you agree to take part in. I will also tell you your planned operation date, where that applies.

I send you a copy of the clinic letter, so that you can read a summary of what we discussed before you sign your consent.

About

The evidence that changes practice worldwide, applied to your care personally.

I am an internationally recognised chest surgeon. Since 2007 I have been a consultant thoracic surgeon at the Royal Brompton Hospital, where I lead the academic department of thoracic surgery, designing and delivering the randomised trials that define how lung cancer, mesothelioma and pleural disease are treated across the world.

That same discipline shapes how I care for each patient: systems-based care, continuously audited, refined for excellent survival outcomes and very low complications — so you are home, confident and healthy in the shortest time.

— Professor Eric Lim, MB ChB, MD, MSc, FRCS (C-Th)

Private consultations at the Royal Brompton Hospital, Chelsea, London, United Kingdom.

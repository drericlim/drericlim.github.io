Coming into hospital

You will usually be admitted at 7am on the day of surgery.

Please do not eat anything from midnight. Drink a glass of water every hour until your surgery.

I will come to see you before your operation, to go through the plan and answer any remaining questions.

Day of surgery

When we call you to theatre, an anaesthetist will meet you and give you a general anaesthetic. We perform your operation, and before you know it you will be awake in the recovery area.

We aim for you to be free of pain from that moment, so if you are uncomfortable please tell the nurse looking after you.

If you wake with a small chest drain, we remove it as soon as it is safe to do so.

You will usually spend a few hours in the recovery area, and then go to the ward.

Going home

We plan for you to go home on the same day. Please do not worry: you will not be discharged if there is any clinical concern, or if you do not feel confident to go.

Before you leave, we make sure you are free of pain and well enough to go.

It takes a little time to get your medicines from the pharmacy, so please allow for this when you arrange your journey home.

  1. Step1

    Coming into hospital

    You will usually be admitted at 7am on the day of surgery.

    Please do not eat anything from midnight. Drink a glass of water every hour until your surgery.

    I will come to see you before your operation, to go through the plan and answer any remaining questions.

  2. Step2

    Day of surgery

    When we call you to theatre, an anaesthetist will meet you and send you off to sleep. We perform your operation, and before you know it you will be awake in the recovery area.

    We aim for you to be free of pain from that moment, so if you are uncomfortable please tell the nurse looking after you.

    You may wake with a small chest drain. We remove it as soon as it is safe to do so.

    You will usually spend a few hours in the recovery area.

  3. Step3

    Going home

    We plan for you to go home on the same day, or later if you have any issue that needs our attention.

    Before you leave, we make sure you are confident, free of pain and well enough to go.

    It takes a little time to get your medicines from the pharmacy, so please allow for this when you arrange your journey home.

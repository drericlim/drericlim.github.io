01My story

Research and patient care, as one job.

EL

I trained in medicine and surgery at the University of Sheffield, then in heart and chest surgery in Cambridge and London. Along the way I held a Medical Research Council scholarship and completed an MSc in Medical Statistics and an MD in surgical research, before qualifying as a specialist cardiothoracic surgeon and focusing entirely on surgery of the chest.

I have been a consultant thoracic surgeon at the Royal Brompton Hospital since 2007 and Professor of Thoracic Surgery at Imperial College London since 2018. Alongside a busy clinical practice, I lead the academic division of thoracic surgery at the Royal Brompton and a lung screening programme for the early detection of lung cancer.

As Chief Investigator I lead three national trials: MARS 2 in mesothelioma, VIOLET in keyhole surgery for lung cancer and RAMON in advanced lung cancer. I also work on blood tests for the early detection of cancer, and teach through the virtual Grand Rounds and my statistics course.

NHS patients

NHS referrals come through your hospital's multidisciplinary team, the group of specialists who review your scans and plan your care. Once we receive your referral from your NHS multidisciplinary team, your scans are transferred to the Royal Brompton and we will contact you with an appointment.

Private patients

You can see me directly, with no referral letter needed. Maria, my practice manager, will arrange a consultation at a time that suits you and guide you through each step, from your first appointment to your recovery.

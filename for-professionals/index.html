VIOLET

VIdeo assisted thoracoscopic lobectomy versus conventional Open LobEcTomy

Is keyhole lobectomy better than open surgery for early-stage lung cancer?

Design
Multicentre randomised trial, UK
Participants
503
Funder
NIHR Health Technology Assessment
Published
NEJM Evidence, 2022

Keyhole surgery gave a better recovery of physical function at five weeks (median score 73 against 67 after open surgery). Serious adverse events after discharge occurred in 30.7% against 37.8%, and at one year there was no difference in progression-free or overall survival.

Read the paper

Published 2024

MARS 2

Mesothelioma and Radical Surgery 2

Does extended pleurectomy decortication help people with pleural mesothelioma?

Design
Phase 3 multicentre randomised trial, 26 UK hospitals
Participants
335
Funder
NIHR Health Technology Assessment; Cancer Research UK
Published
Lancet Respiratory Medicine, 2024

Surgery with chemotherapy was associated with worse survival to two years than chemotherapy alone (median 19.3 against 24.8 months), and with more serious adverse events.

Read the paper

RAMON

RAdical Management Of Advanced Non-small cell lung cancer

After first-line drug treatment for advanced lung cancer, does treating all remaining disease help?

Design
Multicentre randomised trial with internal pilot, UK
Funder
NIHR Health Technology Assessment
Published
Protocol: BMJ Open, 2023

Compared local consolidative treatment (surgery, radiotherapy or ablation) plus maintenance treatment with maintenance treatment alone. The trial closed because it was unable to recruit enough participants.

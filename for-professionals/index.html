03Statistics for clinicians

Statistics, in plain English.

My statistics course for doctors and researchers covers the foundations of clinical research, taught without the mathematics getting in the way.

The full course Statistics for clinicians, online Every module in full, with worked examples from real trials. Learn at your own pace on the course website. Go to the course ↗ (opens in a new tab)
